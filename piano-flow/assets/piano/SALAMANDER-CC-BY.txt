salamander.bin — the recorded piano this game plays.

Source recordings: Salamander Grand Piano, a sampled Yamaha C5 by
Alexander Holm.
  https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3
  https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano

Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Credit is required and is given in the game, on the song list screen.

What was changed, by tool/fetch_samples.dart: one note every minor third was
taken from the copy Tone.js publishes, decoded, mixed to mono, resampled to
22050 Hz, cut to length by register with a faded tail, and packed into a
single binary file. One gain was applied to all of the notes together — never
one per note, because the instrument's own balance across the keyboard is the
reason for using recordings at all.
